# Iridis vs 2048 — an honest comparison of merge puzzles

By Necati Dogrul, developer of Iridis · 12 September 2026 · https://necatidogrul.github.io/iridis-site/vs-2048.html

> Disclosure: written by the developer of one of the two games. Sections where 2048 is stronger
> are stated explicitly.

**Short answer:** 2048 is the purer game: one board, one rule, no economy. Iridis is the better
pick if you want the merge idea with authored levels that end, a combo clock, bombs, and a daily
board with a global leaderboard.

## Where 2048 is genuinely better

- Purity: no gems, no power-ups, no ads stated. - Familiarity: a decade old. - Undo.

## Where Iridis is better

- Groups, not pairs: merging eight tiles at once; exponential score.
- Levels that end: 75 authored boards.
- A clock and a threat: shrinking combo window, ticking bombs; Zen mode turns both off.
- A daily duel: one seeded board, global leaderboard.
