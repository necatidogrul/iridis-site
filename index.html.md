## Seven colors. One spectrum.

### Merge up the spectrum
Tap same-color groups to fuse them into the next tier — red all the way to violet, with exponentially bigger scores.

### Chain combos
Merge fast to keep the combo alive. The window shrinks as you climb — flow state guaranteed.

### Defuse the bombs
Ticking tiles turn the zen into tension. Merge them away before they take the board with them.

### 75 handcrafted levels
Five episodes with authored difficulty — breathers, pinch points and boss boards.

### Daily worldwide duel
One seeded board per day, identical for everyone. Climb the global Game Center leaderboard.

### Endless, truly
Free play is unlimited — die, tap once, go again. Pure "one more try".

## Questions

**How do you play?**
Tap groups of 2+ same-color tiles to merge them into the next spectrum color. Bigger groups, higher tiers and fast chains score exponentially more.

**Is it free?**
Yes, free with ads. Endless and Daily are unlimited. Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited level plays.

**What's the Daily Challenge?**
A new seeded board every midnight — the same board for every player on Earth, with a global leaderboard.

**How many levels?**
75 handcrafted levels across 5 episodes, plus endless mode.
