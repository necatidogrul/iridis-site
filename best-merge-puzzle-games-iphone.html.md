> Twelve merge and sort puzzle games for iPhone compared by US rating count, by type and by
> what they charge you. Written by Necati Dogrul, who develops Iridis — last on this list. That
> conflict of interest is disclosed throughout, and Iridis is placed last because it has the
> fewest ratings.

Updated 12 September 2026. Rating counts read from the US App Store via the public iTunes lookup API.

## The one thing most lists get wrong

"Merge puzzle" is four different games wearing one keyword: number merge (the 2048 family),
sort puzzles (Water Sort, Hexa Sort), merge-2 story games (Merge Mansion) and physics merge (the
watermelon game). Iridis is a fifth, smaller lineage: tap a group of same-colour tiles and the
whole group fuses into one tile of the next colour, so big groups are worth far more than pairs.

The second thing lists skip is what the game charges you: time (energy or lives on a timer),
attention (interstitial ads) or money once. Iridis charges attention in the free tier and money
once to stop.

## How to choose

- Ten minutes of calm: Water Sort Puzzle.
- The original: 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli.
- A long game with a story: Merge Mansion (energy timers are the genre, not a flaw).
- Merge with authored levels, combos and a daily leaderboard: Iridis.

## What to check before you install

- Energy or lives? - Ads between rounds, and is there a one-time remove-ads purchase? - Offline? - Does it end?
