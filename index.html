Is it free?

Yes, free with ads. Endless and Daily are unlimited. Premium removes ads and unlocks unlimited level plays.

What's the Daily Challenge?

A new seeded board every midnight — the same board for every player on Earth, with a global leaderboard.

How many levels?

75 handcrafted levels across 5 episodes, plus endless mode.
