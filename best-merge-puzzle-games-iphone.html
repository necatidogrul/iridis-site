Best merge puzzle games for iPhone (2026)

By Necati Dogrul · Updated 12 September 2026 · US App Store rating counts read via Apple's public iTunes lookup API on 12 September 2026

Disclosure: I develop Iridis, which is last on this list. It is placed last because it has the fewest ratings. The list is ordered by rating count, not by my opinion.

The one thing most lists get wrong

"Merge puzzle" on the App Store is four different games wearing one keyword. Number merge is the 2048 family: tiles double when they meet. Sort puzzles (Water Sort, Hexa Sort) do not merge anything in the 2048 sense; you move things until each container is uniform. Merge-2 story games (Merge Mansion) use merging as the engine of a long meta-game with energy timers and events. Physics merge (the watermelon game) is about dropping and chaos. Iridis is a fifth, smaller lineage: tap a group of same-colour tiles and the whole group fuses into one tile of the next colour, which makes big groups worth far more than pairs.

The second thing lists skip is what the game charges you. Every game here is free to download; the difference is whether it charges time (energy or lives that refill on a timer), attention (interstitial ads between rounds), or money once. A story merge game charges time by design. A sorting game usually charges attention. Iridis charges attention in the free tier and money once to stop.

How to choose

Ten minutes of calm: Water Sort Puzzle (either one). Hundreds of thousands of people cannot be wrong about a nap-time game.

The original: 2048 by Gabriele Cirulli. It has undo and statistics and nothing else, which is the point.

A long game with a story: Merge Mansion. Know that it is built around energy timers; that is the genre, not a flaw.

Merge with authored levels, combos and a daily leaderboard: Iridis. Seventy-five hand-made boards with pinch points and boss boards, a combo window that shrinks as you climb, bombs, and one seeded board a day that is identical for everyone. I built it, so weigh that accordingly.

What to check before you install

  • Energy or lives? If levels refill on a timer, the game is asking for money or patience. Decide which you have.
  • Ads between rounds? Look for a one-time "remove ads" purchase rather than a subscription.
  • Offline? Story games and leaderboards need a connection; pure puzzles should not.
  • Does it end? Authored levels finish; endless modes do not. Both are fine. Know which you want on a flight.
